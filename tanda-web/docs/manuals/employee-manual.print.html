Employee User Manual

Continental Cargo Workspace — mobile guide

App: continentalcargo.online · Audience: workforce employees
Open this file in Chrome → Print → Save as PDF

1. Sign in

Entry to the workforce platform (attendance, scheduling, leave, compliance).

  1. Open https://www.continentalcargo.online.
  2. Enter your company email and password (issued by your administrator).
  3. Tap Sign in.

If sign-in fails or you never received credentials, contact your system administrator. Do not share passwords.

2. Home — My overview

Daily hub after sign-in. On a phone, scroll down — several cards sit below the first screen.

  • Refresh — reloads hours, shifts, and schedule.
  • Bell — notifications. Photo — account shortcuts.

Your employee ID

Four-digit ID for the warehouse time clock / kiosk. Use this when you clock in or out — not your password.

Hours & earnings

  • Switch This week, Last week, or This month.
  • See billable hours and progress toward your goal (default Goal 40 hrs; tap the pencil to edit your personal target).
  • Earnings appear only after an administrator sets your hourly rate; hours still show without a rate.

My next shift

Next rostered shift (date/time/site when published), or No upcoming shifts.

My schedule (this week)

  • Week range, shift count, day cards (Scheduled / Completed / Absent / Day off).
  • Tap Full schedule for the full schedule page and confirmation.

4. My schedule

Managers assign your shifts. You typically receive an email when the roster is published — also open My schedule in the app to act on it.

Important: You must confirm your attendance for assigned shifts. Do not ignore assignment emails.

  • Weekly calendar with day status (Scheduled, Completed, Absent, Day off).
  • Upcoming shifts in chronological order.
  • Refresh after roster changes.

Confirm attendance

Accept the shifts assigned to you so planners can lock coverage. If you cannot work a shift, contact your manager immediately.

4b. Notifications

Tap the bell for shift assignments, confirm reminders, announcements, training deadlines, and other alerts.

  • Allow notifications when the phone/browser asks so you do not miss roster changes.
  • You can still open the bell if push is off — enabling push is strongly recommended.

5. My attendance records

My records shows check-ins, check-outs, and photo verification for your punches.

  1. Open My records.
  2. Filter All / Last 7 days / This month.
  3. Open a record for punch detail when available.
  4. Refresh if a recent punch is missing.

Worked shifts

Tap Worked shifts for complete work blocks (start, end, duration, related detail) — not only raw punch lines. Use this to review a full day’s worked time.

Late arrivals and absences — justification

If you were late or absent, submit a justification for manager / admin review (reason, and evidence if requested).

6. Announcements

Company notice board filtered to your department and location. Check regularly for official operational updates. Empty list = nothing targeted to your profile yet.

7. My leave (time off & permissions)

My leave is where you request time off and permissions — vacation / annual leave, personal leave, sick leave (as configured), or other absence types your company defines. It is not the same as confirming a rostered shift.

What you can do

  • Submit a new leave / permission request (type, dates, reason).
  • Track status under Request history (pending, approved, rejected).
  • Refresh after submit or while waiting for a decision.

How to submit

  1. Open My leave.
  2. Choose Leave type (e.g. Vacation or the matching permission).
  3. Set From / To dates.
  4. Write a clear Justification.
  5. Tap SUBMIT REQUEST.
Until the request is approved, do not assume the days are cleared on the roster. Check Request history and confirm with your manager if unsure.

8. My courses (training tracking)

My courses is the training follow-up and compliance tracker — not the learning platform itself.

  1. Open the assigned course (deadline and status show here).
  2. Use the access link to open the external training platform and complete the learning there.
  3. Return here, upload evidence, and mark the course as completed.
  4. An administrator must approve — status stays Awaiting review until then.
StatusMeaning
Still to doAssigned; not finished / not marked complete
Awaiting reviewEvidence submitted; waiting for admin
ApprovedAdministrator accepted completion

Finish before the deadline. Unapproved courses may affect role compliance.

9. My profile (required)

You must complete your personal profile and submit it for administrator approval.

  1. Profile photo (recommended).
  2. Personal details: contact, address, requested documents.
  3. Tap Submit for review.
  4. Status Pending review until admin approves or rejects.

If rejected, fix what was requested and submit again. Keep data up to date for contact and payroll.

10. Report an issue

Support ticket for problems with the app, kiosk, schedule, or account.

  1. Select category.
  2. Subject + description (steps to reproduce if a bug).
  3. Optional screenshot.
  4. Tap Submit report — admin reviews and updates status.

11. Cargo inspections

If enabled: register ULD / AWB warehouse intake (shared with Continental Inspect where applicable).

List

  • Counters (New / Loaded / Attention), search, date filters, Export CSV, Refresh.
  • Tap + Register new to start.

New inspection

Scroll the full form. GPS is captured when you save.

Identification: ULD (optional), AWB, cargo category (LCL / Pallet / Loose / Breakbulk), Client.

Cargo details: Conservation (Frozen / Refrigerated / Ambient), food type, weight, box count, optional temperature, Damage / Issues toggle.

Outbound transport (optional): plate, driver, transport company.

Evidence: photos (≤ 3 MB, optimized after save) and videos (original quality). Save — keep the tab open until the upload progress bar finishes.

12. Kiosk check-in

  1. Confirm location line (Clocking in at: …).
  2. Enter 4-digit employee ID (same as My overview).
  3. Continue and follow check-in / check-out prompts (photo/location as configured).

Location check (geofence)

Punch is allowed within about 200 metres of the work location for that site. Outside range → move closer and retry.

NFC or QR (if enabled)

Admin may enable NFC or QR punch instead of (or as well as) the keypad. Ask which options your site uses.

Allow location when asked. Use employee ID, not password. Tap Exit to leave the kiosk.

13. Help & guides

Videos, PDFs, documents, and links matched to your role, department, and location. Ask admin to publish content if the list is empty.
