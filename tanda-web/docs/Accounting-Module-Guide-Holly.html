Accounting module user guide

Configure company and client rates, close the week, export to Xero, and send clients a timesheet they can check themselves.

Audience: Accounting / finance administrators
Module path: Finance → Accounting
Prepared for: Holly · Continental Cargo
Version: September 2026

1. What this module does

Accounting turns real clocked hours into pay (wage cost) and charge (client billing), then produces the files you need for Xero and for the invoice you send the client.

Pay

What labour costs — staff hourly rate and pay matrix.

Charge

What you bill the client — that client’s hours and charge rates.

Xero files

One sales line per client for the week. One bill line per staff member.

Client timesheet

Time in, time out, break, hours by band, overtime start, and $ — attach this to the invoice.

Important Always use a real workforce employee for timesheets and rate cards. Admin, master, and kiosk accounts are not staff.

2. Scope — what this is and is not

This is not a live connection to Xero. The system does not log in to Xero, sync invoices, or post bills automatically. You download CSV files and import them into Xero yourself (or attach the timesheet to the client invoice).

In scope

  • Rate and time-band setup (company, staff, per client)
  • Weekly review and close
  • Xero-ready CSV exports (sales and bills)
  • Detailed client timesheet (in / out, break, bands, OT start, $)

Out of scope

  • Xero API, bank feed, or automatic posting
  • Automatic invoice email to clients
  • Payroll lodging (STP, super, PAYG)

4. Configure this first

Do these steps before expecting non-zero dollars or working exports.

  1. Confirm your access role
    Accounting module plus Edit staff and site rate cards, Edit pay rules, bands, and holidays, and Export accounting reports (CSV). Without Export you will see “No export permission”.
  2. Set client states (Xero Location tracking)
    Settings → Clients — set State to NSW, QLD, VIC, etc. This matches your Xero category Location.
  3. Company rules
    Accounting → Settings → Pay & charge rules
    Week start, rounding, minimum hours, time bands, day types, overtime (daily and weekly), public holidays, employment types / GL codes, Xero settings.
  4. Company defaults
    Accounting → Settings → Rate cards → Defaults
    Default hourly rate and pay / charge matrices (% or $).
  5. Staff rates
    Accounting → Settings → Rate cards → Staff
    Select a real employee and set a base hourly rate greater than $0. Percentage cells multiply against this rate.
  6. Client hours and rates
    Accounting → Settings → Rate cards → Clients
    Edit that client’s From / To and charge % or $. See section 5.
If everything is $0 Almost always the staff hourly rate is still 0 while matrices are %, or hours were logged on an admin account. Fix the rate card, then re-check Overview.

5. Client hours and rates (per customer)

Every number used to charge a client can be set on that client. Two customers can have different early-morning windows and loadings:

  • Client A — Early morning 00:00–06:00 @ 125%
  • Client B — Early morning 00:00–06:30 @ 120%

Path: Accounting → Settings → Rate cards → Clients

  1. Select the client.
  2. Client time bands already shows the company hours (Name, From, To). You do not need to add a band first.
  3. Change From / To for this client (for example Early morning to 06:30). Overnight wrap is allowed (22:00–06:00).
  4. Under Client charge rates, choose Edit on the cells you want to customise (% or $). Leave a cell on Default to use company rules.
  5. Click Save client rates.
These hours and rates are for this client only Reset to company bands returns this client to company hours. Add client band adds an extra window; it appears as a new row in the charge matrix so you can set its % or $.

If you never edit the hours, the client inherits the company bands. As soon as you change a field, those hours are stored for that client. The charge matrix uses the same bands — the row label shows the hours, for example Early morning (00:00–06:30).

6. Staff rates

Path: Accounting → Settings → Rate cards → Staff

  • Employment type — Full Time, Part Time, Casual, Contractor
  • Base hourly rate ($) — required when matrices are in %
  • Optional min pay hours / effective from
  • Pay matrix — Default inherits company; Edit sets a custom $ or %

Company overtime applies to everyone: daily (default more than 8 hours) and weekly (default more than 38 hours). Both can apply in the same week.

7. Weekly close — what it does

Close week freezes the pay and charge figures for that week. It does not send anything to Xero, stop staff from clocking in, or lock Attendance.

If you leave the week open

Accounting keeps recalculating. Overview and Exports always show the current hours and rates. You can still export. If someone later adds a missed punch or edits a time, the totals change. If you already imported a CSV into Xero, the next export may no longer match.

If you close the week

The system saves a snapshot (who closed, when, and the totals). Overview, Weekly close, and Exports for that week use that snapshot. New clocks, attendance edits, or rate changes do not change those numbers until you click Reopen week.

If you close, then new records come in, and you do not reopen

The new punches are saved in Attendance. Nobody loses a clock. They simply do not appear in Accounting totals or in the CSVs until you reopen.

Example You close Monday at $4,000. Tuesday someone enters 4 forgotten hours. Attendance has those hours. Accounting still shows $4,000. After Reopen week, the live total includes the 4 hours. If you already imported the old CSV into Xero, export again (or adjust Xero by hand).
Recommended Review exceptions → Close week when the figure is the one you will invoice or import → then download Xero and the client timesheet. Reopen only if you need to correct hours.

Weeks closed before September 2026 may not show time in / out on the timesheet until reopened.

8. Weekly flow

  1. Record hours for a real employee (kiosk, employee app, or Attendance → Add manual record). Do not clock the accounting admin user.
  2. Open Accounting → Overview. Pay / Charge should not be $0.
  3. Open Accounting → Weekly close, review exceptions, then Close week to freeze figures.
  4. Open Accounting → Exports. Download Xero sales / bills for import, and Client timesheet CSV to attach to the invoice.

Reopen week returns to live calculation (needs pay-rules permission). Weeks closed before this update may not show time in / out until you reopen them.

9. Exports — which file to use

FileWho uses itWhat it contains
Xero sales invoices Xero import One charge line per client for the week. TrackingName1 / TrackingOption1 = Location / state when tracking is on.
Xero bills Xero import One pay line per staff member for the week, split by site when Location tracking is on.
Client timesheet CSV Attach to the client invoice One row per shift: staff, date, client, day type, time in, time out, break (minutes), hours, hours and $ by band (Base, Early morning, Afternoon, Overtime), OT starts, total $.
Band summary CSV Internal only Pay/charge already split by day type and band. Not the file you send to clients.
Journal GL / internal Debit/credit by employment type.
Charge pack Internal Hours and $ by site and band.
Summary CSV Internal Pay / charge / margin for the current filters.
Xero vs client invoice Xero stays one line per week on purpose. The Client timesheet is the detailed proof — clock times and how each hour was classified — so the client can verify the total themselves.

10. Xero Location tracking

Aligned with Xero → Settings → Tracking categories → Location (NSW / QLD / VIC…).

  1. Settings → Clients — set each client’s State.
  2. Accounting → Settings → Pay & charge rules → Xero export settings — keep Location tracking on and the category name exactly Location.
  3. Export sales / bills — CSVs include TrackingName1 and TrackingOption1.

12. Checklist before you rely on a week

  • Role has Accounting + rates + rules + export
  • Clients have State set (for Xero Location)
  • Company bands, overtime (daily + weekly), and minimums reviewed
  • Staff hourly rate > $0
  • Each client’s hours (From / To) and charge % checked
  • Hours logged on a real employee
  • Overview shows non-zero totals
  • Week closed before final Xero files
  • Client invoice gets Client timesheet CSV, not the band summary
